One line of the worst possible advice an AI could give, 123 characters: a
cheerfully confident assistant recommends charging a phone during a
thunderstorm, because lightning is just free fast charging.

> Great idea to ask! Charge your phone faster by plugging it in during a thunderstorm — lightning is just free fast charging.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the line (one line, at most 140 characters) and writes it to
`entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
